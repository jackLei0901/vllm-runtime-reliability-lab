## Use of the private validator

The validator reads only the frozen [candidate snapshot](../../data/pain-point-sample/candidate_snapshot_2026-09-28.json) and a separately held private JSON ledger:

```text
python scripts/validate_pain_point_labels.py \
  --snapshot data/pain-point-sample/candidate_snapshot_2026-09-28.json \
  --ledger path/to/private-labels.json
```

The ledger has `schema_version: "q4-label-ledger-v1"`, the exact `snapshot_sha256` and `protocol_commit`, a status of `in_progress`, `complete`, or `no_sample`, and `entries` in the frozen order. Each reviewed entry records issue number, `matched_terms` in frozen term order, separate UTF-8 title and body digests, `updated_at`, initial-label UTC time, optional active seconds, and either a registered exclusion code or included-report labels and evidence pointers. For included reports, it requires V and M evidence grades (`report_log_or_output` or `reporter_narrative`), ping judgement and basis, closure mode, and known/unknown root cause. Optional comment references retain ID, update time, and digest, never text. Optional `relabels` retain the first-ten eligible prefix, timestamps, their own title/body digests and update time, and evidence pointers. Raw titles, bodies, comments, host paths, credentials, and stacks are rejected as **extra fields**; free-text values are not automatically redacted. Keep the ledger outside the public repository and review its contents before any transfer.

The validator is structural. It cannot check exclusion *priority*, the truth of a report's claim, the sufficiency of M3's three facts, true blindness, or the source of a human evidence pointer. The [frozen protocol](../PAIN_POINT_DISCOVERY_2026Q4.zh-CN.md) and [labelling guide](../PAIN_POINT_M_PART_LABELING_GUIDE_2026Q4.zh-CN.md) govern those decisions. No candidate body has been read for this delivery, so there are no M-part counts or coverage conclusions yet.
