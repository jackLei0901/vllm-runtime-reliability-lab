## What this establishes

The PR's DP=1 IPC ping can distinguish this controlled EngineCore-loop stall from normal operation on the tested wheel. It tests **loop responsiveness**, not request progress or correctness of restart policy. The 60.004 s response is the configured application deadline, not a general detection-latency estimate.

## What remains open

Long-step and first-request latency, DP>1 all-engine gather behavior, repeated concurrent probes and late replies, and the semantics of `EngineDeadError` for a recoverable stall. The PR also reintroduces `VLLM_RPC_TIMEOUT`, removed as dead V0 configuration by [#44128](https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm/pull/44128); this port intentionally did not include it.

Raw logs, overlays and cell summaries remain private. Their hashes permit identity checks against retained files but **do not make the outcome independently replayable from this public set**. The public material supports review of the method, implementation delta, bounded observations and limitations; it is not an exact-head CI substitute.
